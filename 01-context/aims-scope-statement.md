# AIMS Scope Statement

## Purpose

This document defines the scope and boundaries of the Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) for EugTech Solutions Ltd.

This supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 4.3, which requires the organisation to determine the scope of the AI management system.

## Organisation

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Organisation | EugTech Solutions Ltd |
| Organisation Type | UK technology consultancy |
| Employees | 50 |
| AIMS Standard | ISO/IEC 42001:2023 |
| Scope Owner | AI Management Representative / Information Security Manager |
| Review Frequency | Annual or after significant AI system change |

## AIMS Scope

The AI Management System of EugTech Solutions Ltd covers the design, deployment, monitoring, and governance of all AI systems used in or provided as part of EugTech's technology consultancy and managed services business.

This includes AI systems used in client service delivery, internal operations, software development support, project estimation, and customer service automation.

## In-Scope AI Systems

| AI System or Use Case | Description | Included in AIMS? |
|---|---|---|
| EugAI customer service chatbot | AI chatbot deployed to client organisations for first-line support | Yes |
| Internal AI tools for project estimation | AI-assisted tools used to estimate project effort, timelines, and resources | Yes |
| AI-assisted code review | AI tools used to support software quality and security review | Yes |
| AI tools for resource planning | Internal AI tools used to support operational planning | Yes |
| AI components in third-party software | AI-enabled tools used by EugTech staff in business operations | Yes |
| Future AI systems | New AI systems developed, procured, or deployed during the scope period | Yes |

## Exclusions

| Exclusion | Justification |
|---|---|
| Personal AI tool use by staff on personal devices for non-work purposes | Outside EugTech operational control |
| Client-owned AI systems where EugTech has no design, deployment, or operational role | EugTech does not control those AI systems |
| AI tools used only for personal learning outside work | Outside business operations and outside AIMS governance boundary |

## Interfaces with Other Management Systems

The AIMS is designed to integrate with existing governance and security practices.

| Framework or System | Relationship |
|---|---|
| ISO 27001 | Information security controls support AI asset protection, supplier management, incident response, and access control |
| NIST AI RMF | Provides AI risk methodology for mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks |
| EU AI Act | Provides regulatory context for AI classification, transparency, and high-risk AI obligations |
| UK GDPR | Applies where AI systems process personal data |

## Scope Boundaries

The AIMS applies to:

- AI governance policies
- AI system ownership and accountability
- AI risk assessment
- AI impact assessment
- AI system monitoring
- AI incident and nonconformity management
- AI competence and awareness
- AI supplier governance
- Annex A AI controls
- Internal audit and management review

## Scope Statement

EugTech Solutions Ltd's Artificial Intelligence Management System applies to all AI systems designed, deployed, managed, or used by the organisation in support of client services and internal business operations.

The AIMS covers the full AI lifecycle, including planning, design, development, procurement, deployment, monitoring, operation, incident response, improvement, and decommissioning.
