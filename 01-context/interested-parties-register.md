# Interested Parties Register

## Purpose

This document identifies interested parties relevant to EugTech Solutions Ltd's Artificial Intelligence Management System.

This supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 4.2, which requires the organisation to understand the needs and expectations of interested parties.

## Interested Parties

| Interested Party | Needs and Expectations | AIMS Response |
|---|---|---|
| Clients | Assurance that AI used in service delivery is fair, accurate, secure, and responsibly governed | Provide AIMS documentation, AI transparency, risk controls, and client-facing assurance |
| Client employees / AI system users | Transparent AI interactions, ability to escalate to humans, fair and accurate responses | Provide AI disclosure, human escalation, monitoring, and fairness testing |
| EugTech employees | Clear AI use policies, training, and protection from inappropriate AI use | Provide AI Policy, competence framework, acceptable use guidance, and reporting channels |
| Board and investors | AI governance that reduces business risk, supports growth, and protects reputation | Provide KPI reporting, management review, internal audit, and risk register updates |
| Regulators | Compliance with UK GDPR, sector-specific guidance, and emerging AI regulation | Maintain risk assessments, impact assessments, incident records, and privacy controls |
| Data Protection Officer | Evidence that personal data risks are identified and controlled | Maintain privacy impact assessment input, data minimisation, retention limits, and incident escalation |
| AI Management Representative / ISM | Clear ownership of the AIMS and evidence of control implementation | Maintain AIMS documentation, audit evidence, risk register, and corrective action tracking |
| AI Engineering Team | Clear technical requirements for safe and secure AI implementation | Provide AI security controls, logging requirements, testing procedures, and lifecycle controls |
| Product Team | AI system performance, user experience, and business value | Provide system ownership, monitoring, KPI tracking, and user feedback processes |
| HR / Training Function | Defined AI competence requirements and training completion evidence | Maintain AI literacy training records and competence framework |
| LLM API Providers | Responsible use of their AI models and compliance with contractual terms | Maintain supplier governance, API terms compliance, and responsible use controls |
| Auditors | Evidence that ISO 42001 clauses and selected Annex A controls are implemented | Provide policies, registers, audit reports, management reviews, and Statement of Applicability |

## Needs and Expectations by Theme

| Theme | Interested Parties | AIMS Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Transparency | Clients, users, regulators | Users must know when they interact with AI |
| Fairness | Clients, users, regulators | Bias and fairness must be monitored |
| Accuracy | Clients, users, product team | AI outputs must be measured and reviewed |
| Privacy | Users, DPO, regulators | Personal data must be minimised and protected |
| Security | Clients, ISM, engineering team | AI systems must be protected from misuse and attack |
| Accountability | Board, auditors, staff | Roles and responsibilities must be clearly assigned |
| Competence | Employees, HR, board | Staff must receive appropriate AI literacy training |
| Supplier assurance | Board, clients, ISM | Third-party AI providers must be assessed |
| Continual improvement | Board, auditors, clients | Incidents, audit findings, and KPIs must drive improvements |

## Communication Requirements

| Interested Party | Communication Method | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Board | AI governance report and KPI dashboard | Quarterly |
| Clients | AI disclosure and assurance documentation | Onboarding and contract review |
| Staff | AI Policy and acceptable use guidance | Onboarding and annually |
| AI system users | AI disclosure notice and human escalation option | At point of interaction |
| Regulators | Incident reports or evidence where required | As required |
| Auditors | AIMS evidence pack | During internal or external audit |
| Suppliers | Supplier reviews and contract discussions | Annually or on major change |

## Key Interested Party Risks

| Risk | Affected Parties | Control Response |
|---|---|---|
| Users do not know they are interacting with AI | AI system users, clients, regulators | AI disclosure and transparency controls |
| AI provides unfair or biased responses | Users, clients, regulators | Bias testing and impact assessment |
| AI processes personal data inappropriately | Users, DPO, regulators | Data minimisation, PII detection, retention controls |
| AI supplier changes model behaviour without notice | Clients, users, product team | Supplier governance and model change monitoring |
| Staff misuse AI tools | Employees, clients, board | AI Policy, competence framework, acceptable use guidance |

## Summary

The interested parties register shows that EugTech's AIMS must address the expectations of clients, users, staff, leadership, regulators, suppliers, auditors, and internal governance teams.

The AIMS responds through policies, risk assessment, AI impact assessment, competence management, transparency controls, monitoring, supplier governance, audit, and continual improvement.
