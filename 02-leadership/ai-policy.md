# AI Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes EugTech Solutions Ltd's commitment to the responsible development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence.

The policy supports the Artificial Intelligence Management System aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

## Policy Statement

EugTech Solutions Ltd is committed to the responsible development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence.

The organisation will maintain an Artificial Intelligence Management System that supports safe, secure, transparent, accountable, fair, and privacy-respecting AI use across client service delivery and internal operations.

## AI Principles

| Principle | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Human-centred | AI must serve human needs and respect human dignity and autonomy |
| Fair and non-discriminatory | AI must not produce systematically biased outcomes affecting protected groups |
| Transparent | Users must know when they interact with AI and understand its limitations |
| Accountable | Clear human accountability must exist for all AI decisions affecting people |
| Safe and secure | AI systems must be protected against misuse, manipulation, and adversarial attacks |
| Privacy-respecting | AI must comply with UK GDPR and minimise personal data processing |
| Continually improving | AI governance must improve through monitoring, audit, review, and corrective action |

## Organisational Commitments

EugTech Solutions Ltd commits to:

- Maintain an AIMS aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023
- Conduct AI impact assessments before deploying new AI systems
- Provide AI literacy training to all staff
- Establish clear AI roles and accountabilities
- Monitor AI system performance and address adverse outcomes promptly
- Comply with applicable AI legislation and regulatory expectations
- Maintain evidence of AI governance, risk assessment, impact assessment, and control implementation
- Review AI risks, incidents, nonconformities, and improvement actions through management review

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- EugAI customer service chatbot
- Internal AI tools for project estimation, code review, and resource planning
- AI components in third-party software used by EugTech staff
- Future AI systems developed, procured, or deployed by EugTech
- Staff, contractors, and teams involved in AI use, governance, monitoring, or support

## Prohibited AI Uses

EugTech AI systems must not be used for:

- Medical advice
- Legal advice
- Financial advice
- Employment decisions without human review
- Credit or eligibility decisions
- Processing sensitive personal data without approval
- Automated decisions affecting individual rights without appropriate governance
- Generating discriminatory, harmful, misleading, or unsafe content

## Human Oversight

Human oversight is required where AI outputs may affect people, clients, service quality, rights, fairness, privacy, or safety.

EugAI must escalate to a human where:

- The query is high-stakes
- The response confidence is low
- The query involves personal data
- The query relates to medical, legal, financial, or employment matters
- The user requests human support
- The AI output could affect customer trust or contractual obligations

## Monitoring and Review

The AI Policy will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After a significant AI incident
- After a major AI system change
- When new legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations apply
- Following internal audit or management review findings
