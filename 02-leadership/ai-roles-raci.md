# AI Roles RACI

## Purpose

This document defines AI governance roles, responsibilities, and accountabilities for EugTech Solutions Ltd.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 5.3, which requires organisational roles, responsibilities, and authorities to be assigned and communicated.

## AI Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | ISO 42001 Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board of Directors | Approve AI Policy. Set AI risk appetite. Receive quarterly AI governance reports. Ultimate accountability. |
| CEO | Ensure adequate resources for the AIMS. Approve AI strategy. Sign the AI Policy. |
| AI Management Representative / ISM | Maintain the AIMS. Report AIMS performance to the board. Coordinate AI risk assessments and audits. |
| Head of Product | AI System Owner for EugAI. Accountable for business outcomes and performance of deployed AI. |
| AI Engineering Lead | Technical implementation of AI controls. Model versioning. Prompt engineering and guardrails. |
| Data Protection Officer | AI-related data protection obligations. UK GDPR compliance. Privacy impact assessments. |
| Department Heads | Ensure staff in their teams comply with the AI Policy. Identify and report AI-related concerns. |
| All Staff | Complete AI literacy training. Use AI only in accordance with policy. Report AI incidents. |

## RACI Key

| Letter | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R | Responsible: Performs the work |
| A | Accountable: Owns the outcome or decision |
| C | Consulted: Provides input |
| I | Informed: Kept updated |

## Governance Notes

- The Board has ultimate accountability for AI governance.
- The CEO ensures resources and leadership support are available.
- The ISM acts as the AI Management Representative and maintains the AIMS.
- The Head of Product owns EugAI as a business system.
- The AI Engineering Lead owns technical AI safeguards.
- The Data Protection Officer owns privacy and data protection considerations.
- Department Heads ensure staff comply with AI requirements.
- All staff must use AI responsibly and report concerns.

## Review

This RACI will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After organisational change
- After a significant AI incident
- After a major change to EugAI or other AI systems
- During management review
