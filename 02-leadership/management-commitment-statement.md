# Management Commitment Statement

## Purpose

This document records leadership commitment to the Artificial Intelligence Management System at EugTech Solutions Ltd.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 5 by demonstrating leadership commitment, accountability, policy approval, resource support, and governance oversight.

## Commitment Statement

EugTech Solutions Ltd is committed to responsible AI governance and the effective implementation of an Artificial Intelligence Management System aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

Leadership recognises that AI can provide business value, improve service delivery, and support innovation. Leadership also recognises that AI can create risks relating to fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, security, accountability, and trust.

The organisation will manage these risks through documented governance, defined responsibilities, risk assessment, AI impact assessment, Annex A controls, monitoring, internal audit, management review, corrective action, and continual improvement.

## Leadership Commitments

Senior management commits to:

| Commitment | Description |
|---|---|
| Establish the AIMS | Maintain an AI Management System aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023 |
| Approve AI Policy | Approve and communicate the AI Policy across the organisation |
| Assign accountability | Define clear AI roles, responsibilities, and authorities |
| Provide resources | Ensure adequate people, tools, training, and governance support |
| Support AI risk management | Ensure AI risks are identified, assessed, treated, and reviewed |
| Support AI impact assessment | Ensure AI systems are assessed for impacts on people, users, and society |
| Promote responsible AI culture | Encourage responsible, transparent, fair, safe, and accountable AI use |
| Monitor performance | Review AI objectives, KPIs, incidents, audit findings, and improvements |
| Ensure compliance | Support compliance with relevant legal, regulatory, contractual, and client expectations |
| Drive continual improvement | Use incidents, nonconformities, audits, and reviews to improve the AIMS |

## Management Responsibilities

| Management Area | Leadership Action |
|---|---|
| AI Governance | Approve policy, scope, risk appetite, and governance roles |
| AI Risk | Ensure AI risks are assessed and treated within risk appetite |
| AI Impact | Require AI impact assessments before deployment or significant change |
| AI Competence | Ensure staff receive appropriate AI literacy and role-specific training |
| AI Monitoring | Review AI performance, bias, accuracy, incidents, and user impact |
| Supplier Governance | Ensure third-party AI providers are assessed and monitored |
| Internal Audit | Support independent review of AIMS implementation |
| Management Review | Review performance and make decisions on improvement actions |
| Continual Improvement | Ensure corrective actions are tracked to completion |
