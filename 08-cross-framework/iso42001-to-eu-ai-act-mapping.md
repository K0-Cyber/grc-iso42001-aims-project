# ISO 42001 to EU AI Act Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps ISO/IEC 42001:2023 to relevant EU AI Act governance and compliance concepts.

The purpose is to show how an AI Management System can support EU AI Act readiness, while recognising that ISO 42001 certification does not automatically guarantee legal compliance.

## Framework Relationship

ISO/IEC 42001 is a voluntary AI Management System standard.

The EU AI Act is a binding AI regulation that applies to AI providers and deployers in scope.

ISO 42001 can support EU AI Act compliance by providing governance evidence, risk management, impact assessment, documentation, monitoring, and continual improvement.

However, EU AI Act compliance may require additional legal and technical documentation beyond ISO 42001.

## EugAI Classification Context

EugAI is a customer service chatbot that interacts with natural persons.

In its current deployment, EugAI is treated as a limited-risk AI system because it provides chatbot support and does not make high-risk decisions.

The main EU AI Act concern is transparency.

EugAI must:

- Inform users they are interacting with AI
- Avoid pretending to be human
- Provide access to human support
- Avoid high-risk use without further assessment

## High-Risk Reclassification Warning

EugAI could become high risk if used for:

- Employment screening
- Promotion or dismissal decisions
- Credit assessment
- Education access or assessment
- Access to essential services
- Biometric identification or categorisation
- Law enforcement
- Migration or asylum decisions
- Decisions significantly affecting legal rights

Before any high-risk use, EugTech would need additional EU AI Act compliance work beyond this ISO 42001 AIMS.

## Important Limitation

ISO 42001 certification or implementation supports EU AI Act compliance evidence, but it does not automatically prove compliance.

The EU AI Act may still require:

- Specific risk classification
- Technical documentation
- Conformity assessment
- EU database registration for certain high-risk systems
- Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment where required
- Serious incident reporting to relevant authorities
- Provider and deployer-specific legal obligations

## Summary

ISO 42001 provides a strong governance foundation for EU AI Act readiness.

For EugTech Solutions Ltd, the AIMS demonstrates responsible AI governance through scope, policy, roles, risk assessment, impact assessment, Annex A controls, audit, management review, incident handling, and continual improvement.
