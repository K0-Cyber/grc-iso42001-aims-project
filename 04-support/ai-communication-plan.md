# AI Communication Plan

## Purpose

This document defines how EugTech Solutions Ltd communicates AI-related information to relevant interested parties.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 7.4, which requires the organisation to determine what to communicate, when to communicate, with whom to communicate, and how to communicate.

## Communication Objectives

The objectives of AI communication are to:

- Ensure staff understand the AI Policy
- Ensure AI users understand AI system capabilities and limitations
- Ensure clients understand where AI is used in service delivery
- Ensure leadership receives AI risk and performance information
- Ensure regulators receive required reports where applicable
- Ensure AI incidents and nonconformities are escalated appropriately
- Support transparency, accountability, and trust in AI use

## Communication Plan

| Audience | What | When | Method | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All Staff | AI Policy and AI acceptable use guidelines | Onboarding and annually | Training session, intranet, policy acknowledgement | HR Manager |
| AI System Users | AI tool-specific guidance, limitations, and escalation routes | Before system use and after major changes | User guidance, system notice, training | Head of Product |
| Clients | AI disclosure, AI capabilities, AI limitations, and human escalation options | Contract onboarding and service updates | Client documentation, portal notice, service review | Customer Experience Manager |
| Board | AI risk report, AIMS KPI dashboard, audit findings, and significant incidents | Quarterly | Board report and management review pack | ISM |
| Regulators | AIMS documentation and AI incident reports where required | As required | Formal regulatory communication | DPO / ISM |
| AI Engineering Team | Technical AI control requirements, logging, testing, and security findings | Project start, release cycle, after incidents | Engineering tickets, security review, team meeting | AI Engineering Lead |
| Department Heads | Team AI responsibilities, training completion, and policy compliance | Quarterly | Management meeting and dashboard | HR Manager / ISM |
| Suppliers | AI governance requirements, contractual expectations, and incident notification requirements | Contract review and annually | Supplier questionnaire and contract review | IT Manager / ISM |

## AI Disclosure Communication

EugAI must clearly disclose that users are interacting with an AI system.

The disclosure should:

- Be shown at the start of the conversation
- Be easy to understand
- Explain that EugAI is an AI assistant
- Explain that human support is available
- Avoid implying the AI is human
- Avoid allowing the AI to deny its AI nature

Example disclosure:

> You are interacting with EugAI, an AI customer service assistant. EugAI can help with common support questions, but a human support agent is available if needed.

## Incident Communication

AI incidents must be communicated based on severity.

| Severity | Internal Communication | External Communication |
|---|---|---|
| High | ISM, CEO, DPO, Head of Product, AI Engineering Lead immediately | Clients, regulators, or suppliers where required |
| Medium | ISM, System Owner, relevant control owner within 1 business day | Clients where service impact occurred |
| Low | System Owner and ISM during routine review | Usually not required |

## Communication Records

The following communication records must be retained:

- AI Policy acknowledgement records
- Training attendance records
- Client AI disclosure evidence
- AI governance reports
- Board reports
- Management review minutes
- Incident communications
- Supplier communications
- Regulatory reports where applicable

## Review

This communication plan will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After a major AI incident
- After changes to AI systems
- After changes to interested party requirements
- During management review
- Following internal audit findings
