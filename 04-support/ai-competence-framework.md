# AI Competence Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the AI competence requirements for EugTech Solutions Ltd.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 7.2, which requires the organisation to determine required competence, ensure people are competent, and retain evidence of competence.

## Scope

This framework applies to:

- All EugTech staff
- AI system users
- AI system owners
- AI technical staff
- AI governance and risk management staff
- Managers responsible for teams using AI
- Staff involved in AI incident reporting, monitoring, or review

## Competence Levels

| Role Level | Required AI Competence |
|---|---|
| All Staff - AI Literacy | Complete AI Literacy Training annually. Understand what AI can and cannot do, how to use AI tools responsibly, how to report AI concerns, and the AI Policy commitments. |
| AI System Users | Complete AI User Training. Understand specific AI tools in their role, when not to use AI, how to verify AI outputs, and escalation procedures. |
| AI System Owners | Complete AI Governance Training. Understand ISO 42001 requirements, AI impact assessment, AI risk management, performance monitoring, and incident response. |
| AI Technical Staff | Complete AI Security Training. Understand secure AI development, prompt engineering security, OWASP LLM Top 10, model versioning, and testing methodology. |
| AI Management Representative / ISM | Complete ISO 42001 Lead Implementer or equivalent. Understand full AIMS requirements, audit methodology, cross-framework alignment, and regulatory landscape. |

## Training Requirements by Role

| Role | Required Training | Frequency | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| All Staff | AI Literacy Training | Annually | Training completion record |
| AI System Users | AI Tool User Training | Onboarding and annually | Training completion record |
| AI System Owners | AI Governance Training | Annually | Certificate or attendance record |
| AI Engineering Staff | AI Security and Secure Development Training | Annually | Training completion record |
| Data Protection Officer | AI Privacy and Data Protection Training | Annually | Training record |
| ISM / AI Management Representative | ISO 42001 implementation or audit training | Every 3 years or on major standard change | Certificate or CPD evidence |
| Department Heads | AI Policy and team governance responsibilities | Annually | Attendance record |

## Core AI Literacy Topics

All staff must understand:

- What AI is and what AI is not
- Common AI limitations
- Hallucination risk
- Bias and fairness risk
- Privacy and personal data risks
- Security risks such as prompt injection
- When not to use AI
- How to verify AI-generated outputs
- How to report AI incidents or concerns
- EugTech AI Policy requirements

## AI User Training Topics

AI system users must understand:

- Approved AI tools
- Approved AI use cases
- Prohibited AI uses
- Human escalation rules
- AI disclosure requirements
- Output verification
- Handling personal data
- Reporting poor AI outputs
- Recording AI-related concerns

## AI System Owner Training Topics

AI system owners must understand:

- ISO 42001 AIMS requirements
- AI impact assessment
- AI risk assessment
- KPI monitoring
- AI incident management
- Supplier considerations
- Management review inputs
- Continual improvement responsibilities

## AI Technical Staff Training Topics

AI technical staff must understand:

- Secure AI development
- Prompt injection risks
- Jailbreak risks
- OWASP LLM Top 10
- Model version control
- Logging requirements
- AI system testing
- Data quality controls
- AI system monitoring
- Secure use of APIs and credentials

## Competence Evidence

The following evidence must be retained:

| Evidence Type | Owner | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| Training completion records | HR Manager | 3 years |
| Role-specific training records | Department Heads | 3 years |
| ISO 42001 training certificates | ISM | 3 years |
| AI security training records | AI Engineering Lead | 3 years |
| Competence review records | HR Manager | 3 years |
| Audit evidence | ISM | 3 years |

## Competence Review

AI competence will be reviewed:

- Annually
- When a new AI system is introduced
- When an AI system changes significantly
- After an AI incident
- After internal audit findings
- When legal or regulatory requirements change

## Competence Gaps

If a competence gap is identified, EugTech will:

- Assign additional training
- Restrict AI access where needed
- Provide role-specific guidance
- Update training materials
- Track completion
- Report unresolved gaps to management review
