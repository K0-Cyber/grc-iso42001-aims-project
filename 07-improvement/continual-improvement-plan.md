# Continual Improvement Plan

## Purpose

This document records planned improvements to EugTech Solutions Ltd's Artificial Intelligence Management System.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 10.2, which requires the organisation to continually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the AIMS.

## Improvement Plan

| Improvement Area | Action | Owner | Target Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI literacy training | Build mandatory AI ethics module. Increase training to 2 hours total. Add assessment at end. | HR Manager | Q2 2026 | Planned |
| KPI dashboard | Develop Power BI dashboard feeding from AI system logs automatically. Remove manual data entry. | ISM | Q2 2026 | Planned |
| Supplier governance | Develop formal AI supplier assessment scoring matrix. Create annual reassessment calendar. | IT Manager / ISM | Q2 2026 | Planned |
| AI impact assessment | Create standardised impact assessment template. Train system owners on the process. | ISM | Q2 2026 | Planned |
| Logging completeness | Implement centralised AI log aggregation to Sentinel workspace. Enable KQL queries. | AI Engineering Lead | Q3 2026 | Planned |
| Phase 2 AI expansion | Conduct AI impact assessment for new client sectors before EugAI expansion. | Head of Product / ISM | Before expansion | Planned |

## Improvement Drivers

Continual improvement actions are driven by:

- Internal audit findings
- Management review decisions
- AI incidents
- Nonconformities
- KPI results
- Supplier assurance gaps
- Regulatory changes
- AI system changes
- User feedback
- Lessons learned from monitoring and testing

## Improvement Prioritisation

| Priority | Criteria |
|---|---|
| High | Reduces significant AI risk, regulatory exposure, security risk, or user harm |
| Medium | Improves AIMS effectiveness, monitoring, assurance, or competence |
| Low | Improves documentation, efficiency, or reporting maturity |
