# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AIMS Portfolio Project

## Project Overview

This project implements an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) for EugTech Solutions Ltd, a simulated 50-person UK technology consultancy using AI in client service delivery and internal operations.

The project is based on ISO/IEC 42001:2023, the international standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems.

The aim of this project is to demonstrate how an organisation can govern AI responsibly through documented scope, leadership commitment, AI risk assessment, AI impact assessment, competence management, Annex A controls, internal audit, management review, and continual improvement.

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Define the scope and boundaries of the AI Management System
- Identify internal and external context affecting AI governance
- Document interested parties and their AI-related needs
- Establish an AI Policy aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023
- Define AI roles, responsibilities, and governance accountability
- Assess AI risks using likelihood, impact, residual risk, and ownership
- Complete an AI Impact Assessment for EugAI
- Define AI objectives and KPIs
- Establish an AI competence and communication framework
- Document selected Annex A controls in a Statement of Applicability
- Produce an internal audit report and management review minutes
- Record nonconformities, AI incidents, corrective actions, and continual improvement
- Map ISO 42001 to ISO 27001, NIST AI RMF, and the EU AI Act
