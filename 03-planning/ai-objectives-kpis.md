# AI Objectives and KPIs

## Purpose

This document defines measurable AI objectives and key performance indicators for EugTech Solutions Ltd's Artificial Intelligence Management System.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 6.2, which requires AI objectives to be established, monitored, and reviewed.

## AI Objectives Summary

| AI Objective | KPI | Target | Measurement Method | Owner | Review Frequency |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Responsible AI deployment | AI impact assessment completion rate | 100% | Percentage of in-scope AI systems assessed before deployment | ISM | Quarterly |
| Fair AI outputs | Bias test pass rate | 100% | Percentage of quarterly bias tests passed | Customer Experience Manager | Quarterly |
| Accurate AI outputs | EugAI factual accuracy | 95% or higher | Monthly accuracy testing against approved knowledge base | Head of Product | Monthly |
| Transparent AI | AI disclosure compliance | 100% | Percentage of AI interactions displaying AI disclosure notice | Head of Product | Monthly |
| Secure AI | High-severity AI security incidents | 0 | AI incident register and security monitoring | AI Engineering Lead | Monthly |
| Staff AI competence | AI literacy training completion | 100% | Training completion records | HR Manager | Monthly until complete, then quarterly |
| Governance effectiveness | AIMS internal audit findings resolved | 100% by due date | Corrective action tracker | ISM | Quarterly |

## KPI Reporting

KPI performance will be reported through:

- Monthly operational review
- Quarterly AI governance report
- Internal audit
- Management review
- Corrective action tracking
- Board reporting where required

## Management Review Input

The KPI results are used to support:

- AIMS performance evaluation
- Leadership review
- AI risk register updates
- Continual improvement planning
- Resource allocation
- Training prioritisation
- Supplier governance decisions
