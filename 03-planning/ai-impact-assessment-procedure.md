# AI Impact Assessment Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines when and how EugTech Solutions Ltd performs AI Impact Assessments.

It supports ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Clause 6.1.4 by ensuring AI systems are assessed for their potential impact on people, groups, users, clients, and society.

## Scope

This procedure applies to:

- New AI systems
- Existing AI systems undergoing significant change
- AI systems used in client service delivery
- Internal AI tools affecting work, pricing, planning, or decisions
- Third-party AI tools used by EugTech staff
- AI systems that may affect people, clients, fairness, privacy, transparency, or rights

## Assessment Triggers

| Trigger | When an AI Impact Assessment Is Required |
|---|---|
| New AI system | Before any new AI system is placed into service |
| Significant system change | When training data, model version, deployment context, or intended use changes |
| Adverse outcome detected | After any incident where AI produced harmful, unfair, inaccurate, or unsafe outputs |
| Scope expansion | When AI is used for new purposes or with new user groups |
| Annual review | All deployed AI systems are reassessed annually |
| Regulatory change | When legal or regulatory obligations materially change |
| High-risk use proposal | Before AI is used in employment, credit, education, healthcare, or essential services contexts |

## AI Impact Assessment Process

### 1. Identify the AI System

Document:

- AI system name
- System owner
- Intended purpose
- Users
- Deployment context
- AI technology used
- Data processed
- Third-party dependencies

### 2. Identify Affected Parties

Identify individuals, groups, or organisations that may be affected, including:

- Direct users
- Client organisations
- Data subjects
- Employees
- Vulnerable groups
- Regulators
- Third-party suppliers
- Wider society where relevant

### 3. Assess Positive Impacts

Document expected benefits such as:

- Faster service delivery
- Improved availability
- Better consistency
- Reduced manual workload
- Improved decision support
- Better client experience

### 4. Assess Negative Impacts

Assess possible impacts relating to:

- Accuracy
- Fairness
- Bias
- Privacy
- Security
- Transparency
- Autonomy
- Overreliance
- Accessibility
- Human oversight
- Reputational harm

### 5. Consider Vulnerable Groups

Assess whether the AI system may affect vulnerable individuals or groups, including:

- Healthcare-related users
- Financially vulnerable users
- Users with low technical literacy
- Users with limited language proficiency
- Protected groups under equality legislation
- Employees affected by AI-supported processes

### 6. Define Controls and Monitoring

Document mitigations such as:

- Human escalation
- AI disclosure
- Bias testing
- Accuracy testing
- Privacy controls
- Security testing
- Supplier controls
- Incident response
- Periodic reassessment

### 7. Approve or Reject Deployment

The AI system may be:

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Approved | AI system may be deployed with existing controls |
| Approved with conditions | AI system may be deployed only with monitoring or extra controls |
| Deferred | Deployment delayed until further assessment or mitigation |
| Rejected | AI system must not be deployed in proposed form |

## Required Assessment Fields

Each AI Impact Assessment must include:

- System name
- System owner
- Assessment date
- Intended purpose
- Affected populations
- Potential positive impacts
- Potential negative impacts
- Vulnerable group considerations
- Rights and equality considerations
- Privacy considerations
- Controls and mitigations
- Monitoring conditions
- Approval decision
- Review date

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| AI Management Representative / ISM | Owns the AI impact assessment process |
| System Owner | Provides system purpose, use case, and operational context |
| Data Protection Officer | Reviews privacy and personal data impacts |
| AI Engineering Lead | Provides technical information and control details |
| Head of Product | Reviews business and user impact |
| CEO / Board | Reviews high-risk or strategically significant AI systems |

## Review Requirements

AI Impact Assessments must be reviewed:

- Annually
- After a significant AI system change
- After an AI incident
- After material regulatory change
- Before expansion into new sectors, users, or use cases

## Evidence Produced

The procedure produces:

- Completed AI Impact Assessment
- Approval decision
- Control and monitoring conditions
- Risk register updates
- Management review input
- Internal audit evidence
